Features

Infinite Scrolling

Seamlessly scroll in either direction with content that loops indefinitely. Perfect for discovery-focused experiences.

Progress Indicator

The progress bar at the top shows your current position within the current content loop, providing context during navigation.

Theme Toggle

Switch between light and dark themes with a fixed position button that remains accessible regardless of scroll position.

Example Use Cases

Content Discovery

Create infinite content feeds where users can continuously discover new items without hitting pagination barriers.

Storytelling

Build narrative experiences with looping content that reinforces circular storytelling concepts or repeating themes.

Product Showcases

Highlight product categories in a continuous flow that encourages exploration without dead ends.

Implementation Details

Fixed Elements

Elements like the theme toggle, page indicator, and progress bar are placed outside the looping content to ensure they remain accessible.

Performance Optimization

The library efficiently manages DOM elements, removing clones when they're no longer needed to prevent memory issues during extended browsing.

Event Handling

Event handlers are preserved in the original content and properly delegated to cloned elements to maintain interactivity.
